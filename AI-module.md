Inventory & Warehouse Operations
Tracks real-time stock levels, warehouse locations, and replenishment pipelines.
•⁠  ⁠Real-Time Stock Tracking: Real-time quantity-on-hand (QOH) per warehouse and per format.
•⁠  ⁠Stock Allocation & Reservations: Temporary stock hold when a user enters the checkout funnel (e.g., reserving a copy for 15 minutes before expiration).
•⁠  ⁠Threshold Alerts: Automated alerts and dashboard flags when inventory reaches low-stock reorder points.
•⁠  ⁠Backorder & Pre-Order Logic: Support for accepting pre-orders for upcoming releases or backorders for out-of-stock titles, with automated fulfillment triggers upon restocking.
